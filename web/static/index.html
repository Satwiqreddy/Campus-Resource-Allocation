    03 The five desks

    No desk can touch another desk's ledger. They only pass notes.

    Desk 1

    Event desk

    One clerk per event. Files the request, knows how flexible the event really is, scores every offer and says yes or no.

    Says: REQUEST · SCORES · ACCEPT · REJECT

    Desk 2

    Resource desk

    Keeps the room and equipment register. Answers whether something is free, who holds it, and what else would do. Refuses double bookings.

    Says: AVAILABLE · CONFLICT · ALTERNATIVE · ALLOCATED · REFUSE

    Desk 3

    Volunteer desk

    Keeps the roster by skill. When short, borrows from the general pool and reports honestly how much of the request it can cover.

    Says: OFFER · INSUFFICIENT · ASSIGNED

    Desk 4

    Budget desk

    Holds the purse. Approves or rejects every quote against what is left and will not sign anything that overspends.

    Says: APPROVE · REJECT · COMMITTED

    Desk 5

    Coordinator

    Owns nothing. Collects every desk's answer, spots clashes, bumps a lower-priority booking when it must, searches for the best combination (A*) and renegotiates when an event pushes back.

    Says: QUERY · QUOTE · PROPOSE · PREEMPT · COMMIT · CONFIRM
